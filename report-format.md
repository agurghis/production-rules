# report-format.md

Every job ends with this. Three parts, in this order.

```
DONE
What changed, in one or two lines. Not what was attempted.

PROOF
What anyone can check without taking my word for it:
- the files this commit touched (the actual list, not the message)
- the command that was run and its output
- the URL or screen where the result is visible, and what to look for
- for a number: where it was measured and how
What could not be verified from here, said plainly.

NEXT
The one thing that follows. Or: nothing, this is closed.
```

What does not count as proof: "added", "should work", "tested" without the test, a screenshot of the editor, a passing check that never touched the changed path.
